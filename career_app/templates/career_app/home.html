Empowering Futures Through Premium Career Services

Dr Vivian Mark Williams
Centre Director

The Federal College of Education (Technical) Omoku Career Service Centre is a pivotal initiative of the Tertiary Education Trust Fund (TETFUND) designed to bridge the gap between tertiary institutions and the dynamic job market. The center enhances employability by equipping graduates with the skills, guidance, and resources to thrive in their professional careers. At Career Service Centre, Federal College of Education (Technical) Omoku, we are dedicated to guiding students and graduates toward their career aspirations. With resources, mentorship, and opportunities, we help students and professionals successfully navigate the competitive employment landscape toward a fulfilled future. Our mission is to provide a structured and accessible career support system, ensuring students and recent graduates can successfully navigate the competitive job landscape with confidence.

Key Services

Career Counseling and Guidance

Personalized counseling to identify your strengths, interests, and aspirations, helping you make informed career decisions.

Partnering with industry leaders to provide job and internship opportunities that offer practical experience and meaningful employment.

Workshops and Training Programs

Enhance your employability skills with resume building, interview preparation, personal branding, and communication training.

Networking Opportunities

Entrepreneurship Support

Access to Career Resources
